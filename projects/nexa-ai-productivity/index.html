What is NEXA?

NEXA is an AI productivity app concept presented as a customizable marketing template. This demo is not a live productivity service.

Can I try the app in this demo?

You can explore Planner, Notes, and Focus previews. Sample tasks, AI summaries, and timers are illustrative, not connected to a real app.

Are the prices real?

No. Every plan and price is illustrative demo content. There is no checkout, subscription, or payment processing.

Does NEXA connect to my calendar or notes?

Not in this template. Calendar sync, reminders, AI planning, and collaboration are product concepts. No personal information is collected.

Can I customize this template for my own app?

Yes. Update the design tokens, copy, product visuals, and plans in the included HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Is there an iOS or Android app to download?

There is no published NEXA app linked to this demo. App-store badges are intentionally omitted.
